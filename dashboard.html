Singapore workplace fatalities rise in first half of 2026 as Ministry of Manpower reviews tougher safety measures

MOM reported 21 workplace deaths in Singapore in H1 2026, an annualised fatal injury rate of 1.1 per 100,000 workers, with construction recording 69 fatal and major injuries (a rate of 24.1, the sector's lowest since 2020) even as smaller-scale renovation works saw a slight uptick. The ministry is reviewing a strengthened safety demerit points system and the bizSAFE programme, and has launched a Platform Worker Safety Workgroup alongside an expanded Multi-Agency Taskforce to tackle vehicular risks.

Ministry of Manpower·2026-08-28

MOM to make four heat stress protection measures mandatory for outdoor workers from December 2026

MOM announced that cool drinking water, ice packs, breathable clothing and heat training will become mandatory workplace requirements for outdoor workers, alongside hourly rest breaks when the Wet Bulb Globe Temperature hits 32 degrees C. The measures, which affect construction and other outdoor sectors, take effect from 1 December 2026.
