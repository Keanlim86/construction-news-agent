MOM to make four heat stress protection measures mandatory for outdoor workers from December 2026

MOM announced that cool drinking water, ice packs, breathable clothing and heat training will become mandatory workplace requirements for outdoor workers, alongside hourly rest breaks when the Wet Bulb Globe Temperature hits 32°C.
